## Phase 14, Legal / entity / insurance (research framing)

Deliverables: a **legal-document register** (3 tiers), an **entity-structure briefing**,
and an **insurance briefing**. For the latter two: **inform, don't decide**, give
options + the exact questions to bring to the attorney/broker.

Key research anchors that recur:
- **Venue-not-seller + Section 230** (*Hiam v. HomeAway*, D. Mass. 2017): the ToS must disavow pre-screening/warranties consistently across all docs.
- **The platform enforces/records; counsel drafts.** Map each legal doc to the code that enforces it (e-sign -> Dropbox Sign + `agreement_signed_at`; fee disclosure -> quote API; tax -> ledger; permits -> publish-gating CHECK).
- **Jurisdiction tax is the regulatory monster**, research before the money flow. Marketplace-facilitator statutes determine whether the platform or the host remits; that choice lives in the owner agreement and may require a registration form (Texas: §156.054 + Form AP-102).
- **Entity:** DBA (no separation, avoid for money-handling) vs standalone LLC vs holding-co+subsidiary (the holding co holds no operating assets). Money-handling ⇒ isolation.
- **Insurance:** platform insures operations (Tech E&O + Cyber + CGL $1M/$2M, + management E&O for a managed tier); property is the owner's (require commercial STR coverage in the owner agreement). Note coastal perils (wind/flood) where relevant.
